# Additional Researcher Resources   
The following resources provide additional guidance and examples to help researchers conceptualize their AVAnnotate projects.

- This [descriptive guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Rou29JsV8e2hmj2MLf8gPolYCZgX43jc_vRObgp6OoI/edit?usp=drive_link) traces the process of creating an AVAnnotate project, from conception to final publication. The researcher shares guiding principles and lessons learned along the way help other users in navigating their own projects.

- This [project planning narrative](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Yf2UIh9vmOlZzWWeF-Op1C0LrsvNQKKdqJg5un3_86Y/edit?usp=sharing) outlines some of the planning and research processes for the final AVAnnotate project about [Leslie Flint’s mediumship](https://saamturner.github.io/flint/pages/sound-as-rhetorical-assemblage/). It includes the researcher’s early observations about the audio recordings; the research grid used to organize the work; and drafts of contextual essays that were added as custom pages for the final AVAnnotate project. 

- This [resource](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1KuyKK83KUKkQjmAnEXoZzrwK5yBOScRXUuuMY8a0-hk/edit?gid=0#gid=0) lists example annotation types, index terms, and codes grouped by media format. Researchers may use this table to conceptualize how they might describe and classify their own projects.
